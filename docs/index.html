KhanClipping-Engine

Automated tool that generates, reviews, and schedules short-form narrated video content for personal TikTok accounts.

About

KhanClipping-Engine is a personal automation tool built and operated by a single individual for their own TikTok content accounts. It generates short-form narrated videos, queues them for review, and publishes them to connected accounts on the operator's behalf.

It is not a public product or service offered to other users. No third-party accounts can be created, and access is not available outside the operator's own use.
